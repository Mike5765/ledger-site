Ledger

A personal morning dashboard that runs only on its owner's Mac.

What it is

Ledger answers one question each morning: what do I need to know today? It puts the owner's money, email, calendar, tasks, habits, weather, and news headlines on one screen.

How it works

  • Ledger runs as a small local app on one Mac and is only reachable from that Mac.
  • It reads the owner's own accounts, including Gmail and Google Calendar with read-only access, and keeps what it reads on that Mac.
  • There is no Ledger server, no sign-up, and nothing to download here. Ledger is not offered to the public; it is used by one person, its owner.
